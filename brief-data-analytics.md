# Data Analytics Brief

Box Box Analytics is an online video channel planning its content strategy and wants to understand what the
data says about the channel's performance so far. You are the analyst on the engagement.

## Core requirements

Explore the data in `source-data/` and prepare a short presentation for the stakeholders
described in the [assignment README](README.md).

Your submission must include:

1. **A stakeholder deck** (5 to 10 slides, PDF preferred) in the `results/` folder,
   written for the stakeholders, not for other analysts.
2. **Your workings** in the `solution/` folder: the notebook, scripts or queries that
   produced every number and chart in the deck, plus a `README.md` explaining how to
   reproduce them if applicable. This does not have to be clean, production-ready code,
   ad-hoc working-out is fine.

Your deck must address these standing questions:

- Founder: "What is actually driving our growth, and what should we double down on
  for 2026?"
- Founder: "Champion content feels like our bread and butter. Should we invest in a
  dedicated champion series?"
- Commercial lead: "We are pitching sponsors for 2026. What evidence-backed story about
  our audience's appetite can we tell?"

Requirements:

- Present at least two or three distinct, defensible insights with supporting evidence.
- Be explicit about data limitations and how you handled any imperfections you found.
- Recommendations must follow from the evidence, not from motorsport intuition.

Expect a follow-up conversation: at the debrief interview the stakeholders will ask
questions about your analysis and your recommendations.

## Stretch requirements

- Where two explanations for a pattern are entangled, separate them and show your method.
- Propose a measurement plan for 2026: what the channel should track, and which KPIs
  would tell them the strategy is working.

## Evaluation criteria

Validity and depth of insights; quality of the narrative for the stated audience; visual
communication; technical rigour, including treatment of data quirks; performance in the
debrief discussion.
